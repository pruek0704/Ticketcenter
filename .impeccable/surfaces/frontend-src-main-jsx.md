# TicketCenter expanded service workspace
Mode: Operate. A focused extension and palette revision, authorized by the user: three additional departments, replace dark green, instant messages across windows and more space in the pictured queue/conversation. Preserve the familiar FreeScout-style inbox, readable Thai, role permissions and ordinary forms. Code-first; no comp round or new-world tournament applies.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Put the conversation and next service action within reach, with five real scoped service queues.
OWN-WORLD: Keep contemporary office wayfinding and standard service-desk controls. The user rejects forest green; blue action/selection and slate navigation replace it, with neutral gray chat ground and semantic status colors retained. This explicit palette change supersedes the former DESIGN.md palette, not its task grammar.
STORY: Employee submits to IT, HR, facilities, finance or procurement. Backend routes and enforces the agent's department. Messages are saved, delivered through an authenticated event stream and fetched under the same REST permissions. Reconnection takes a fresh snapshot.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact header, summary tabs and reduced outer margins leave more vertical workspace. The desktop chat feed reserves at least 320px for roughly three short messages; its conversation reserves 500px including the composer. Request details, conversation and activity remain in normal flow within one scrolling detail body, so opening either optional section never squeezes the chat or overlaps its composer. Short windows scroll to reach the composer or activity. A labeled expansion button hides peripheral navigation while retaining an exit. Compact screens show list/detail with a back action.
FORM: Self-hosted Noto Sans Thai and the existing SVG icon grammar. Blue/slate light/dark tokens, ordinary forms/selects/tables. Five full Thai department labels; IT/HR/FAC/FIN/PROC are compact monograms. Live connection state and unread badges provide feedback without a reload.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
